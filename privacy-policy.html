Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information A.T International (“we”, “us”) collects through atinternational.online, what we do with it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are

A.T International is a digital solutions company based in Srinagar, Kashmir, India. You can reach us at info@atinternational.online or on +91 60055 61058.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you send a message through the contact form, we receive the details you type in: your name, your email address, your phone number (optional) and your message. If you email or call us directly, we receive whatever you choose to share with us.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time. We use these logs only to keep the site secure and working.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes and proposals you ask for.
  • To keep the website secure and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply.

We do not use your details for automated decisions, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools to follow you around the web. If that changes, we will update this page first.

5. Third parties

  • Web hosting. The site is hosted by our hosting provider, which stores the website files and processes the server logs mentioned above.
  • Email. Contact form messages are delivered to our business mailbox, so they also pass through our email provider.
  • Google Fonts. The site loads the DM Sans typeface from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font from Google, which can see your IP address. Google’s handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.
  • Links to other sites. Our portfolio links to websites we have built. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records, and then we delete them. You can ask us to delete your details sooner at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can also withdraw your consent to us contacting you. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To make a request, email info@atinternational.online. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, including using a secure (HTTPS) connection and limiting who can read enquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Please check back occasionally.
